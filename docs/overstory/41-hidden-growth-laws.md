# Hidden Growth Laws

## Law 1 - Better seams increase ambition

`🎯 9   🛡️ 8   🧠 4`

Because the runtime seam is strong, the project keeps widening runtime support.
That is natural, but it creates parity pressure.

## Law 2 - Local durability tempts federation

`🎯 10   🛡️ 9   🧠 5`

Because local stores are good, the product naturally wants to stretch into multi-repo orchestration.
That is where the real federation problem appears.

## Law 3 - Prompt clarity can hide runtime softness

`🎯 10   🛡️ 9   🧠 5`

The cleaner the prompt/role library becomes, the easier it is to overestimate real behavioral closure.

## Law 4 - Cleanup improvements do not equal engine maturity

`🎯 9   🛡️ 8   🧠 4`

A system can become much easier to clean, stop, and repair without becoming a durable orchestration engine in the `goclaw` sense.

## Law 5 - Transitional substrate wants to become permanent

`🎯 10   🛡️ 9   🧠 5`

If headless is the future but tmux works well enough today, the project will keep paying substrate tax unless it actively kills the transition.

## Architect takeaway

**`overstory` is full of intelligent local decisions. Its hidden growth laws explain why the repo gets stronger and riskier at the same time.**
