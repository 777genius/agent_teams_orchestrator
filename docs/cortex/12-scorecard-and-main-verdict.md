# Scorecard And Main Verdict

## Main verdict

Cortex is not a toy.

It is also not yet a calm platform-grade orchestration engine.

It currently sits in a valuable middle zone:

- early
- serious
- ambitious
- technically interesting
- still structurally softer than its ideas

## Plain-language conclusion

If you want:

- boring durable engine reference: look first at GoClaw
- daring distributed-runtime ideas: Cortex is one of the most useful studies

## Final read

Cortex is worth studying hard.

Just do not mistake:

- architectural imagination

for:

- already-proven engine maturity
